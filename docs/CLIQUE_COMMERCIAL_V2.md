# BERYL CLIQUE — THE COMMERCIAL · v2
### Working title: *"The Faces Behind the Machine"*
**Runtime:** ~95 seconds · 8 scenes · Runway Seedance / Gen-3 (≤15s each)
**Purpose:** Plays on load at berylize.com. Built to close investors.
**Format:** 16:9, cinematic, photorealistic. Color arc: cold blue faceless dread → warm gold human presence.

_Supersedes CLIQUE_COMMERCIAL.md ("It Was Supposed to Be Easier"). Preserves the
emotional arc (dread → turn → friendship). Reframes the villain from "complexity" to
**faceless, hard-to-talk-to AI agents**, and reframes the hero as a **named,
face-to-face four-person team you speak to like people** — introduced by Amanda._

---

## 🎯 THE BIG IDEA (v2)

> **AI agents were supposed to help. Instead you got a wall of terminals, dashboards,
> and nameless bots. Agents you can't see. Agents you can't talk to. Agents that
> forget who you are the moment the tab closes.**
>
> Then Amanda walks into the room, looks you in the eye, and says: *"Hey — I'm Amanda.
> Tell me what you're building."* And the wall goes away.

The villain isn't a competitor — we never name one. **The villain is the *faceless
agent*: the CLI wrapper you have to wrestle, the config file you have to fight, the
disembodied voice that starts every conversation from zero.** The hero is the most
human thing imaginable — **a real face, a real name, on realtime-live listening,
who already knows you.**

**Two core themes, woven throughout (v2):**
1. **Agents with faces.** Every member of your Clique has a name, a face, a voice, and
   a lens — India, Jeff, Nu, and Amanda who leads. You don't prompt them. You talk
   to them.
2. **Amanda leads. The team joins by name.** Every meeting starts with Amanda alone —
   she listens, she gets it, and *she* brings in the exact teammate the work needs.
   No routing, no @-mentions, no config. Just the four of them, on realtime-live
   listening, ready the second she says a name.
