# BERYL CLIQUE — THE COMMERCIAL
### Working title: *"The Last Lonely Developer"*
**Runtime:** ~95 seconds · 8 scenes · Runway Seedance (≤15s each)
**Purpose:** Plays on load at berylize.com. Built to close investors.
**Format:** 16:9, cinematic, photorealistic. Color arc: cold blue isolation → warm gold belonging.

---

## 🎯 THE EMOTIONAL ARC

| Act | Scenes | Feeling | Color |
|-----|--------|---------|-------|
| **I — The Pain** | 1–3 | Isolation, frustration, "this makes no sense" | Cold teal/blue, 2 AM |
| **II — The Turn** | 4–5 | A flicker of hope. Warmth enters. | Blue → gold bloom |
| **III — The Belonging** | 6–8 | Connection, energy, a real team. The reveal. | Full royal gold |

The whole film is one man's face going from *defeated* to *alive*. That's the sale. Investors don't buy features — they buy the **before/after of a human being.**

## 🎵 SOUND DESIGN

- **0:00–0:24** — Sparse, melancholy solo piano. A single repeating note. Diegetic: keyboard clacks, a frustrated exhale, distant city hum, a clock ticking.
- **0:25–0:36** — Piano holds on a suspended chord. The room goes quiet. *(The "turn.")*
- **0:37–1:06** — Warm strings enter and build. Rhythm picks up. Layered voices/agents murmuring, light laughter, the energy of a real meeting.

- **1:32–1:40** — Everything drops to one warm sustained chord under the logo. A single gold "shimmer" chime on the wordmark.
