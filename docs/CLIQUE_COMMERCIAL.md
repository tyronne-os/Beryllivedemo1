# BERYL CLIQUE — THE COMMERCIAL
### Working title: *"It Was Supposed to Be Easier"*
**Runtime:** ~95 seconds · 8 scenes · Runway Seedance (≤15s each)
**Purpose:** Plays on load at berylize.com. Built to close investors.
**Format:** 16:9, cinematic, photorealistic. Color arc: cold blue isolation → warm gold belonging.

---

## 🎯 THE BIG IDEA

> **AI was supposed to make things easier. Instead it gave us more code, more configs, more dread.**
>
> Beryl Clique throws all of it away. **No keyboard. No chat box.** Just an old-fashioned,
> face-to-face conversation with co-workers — who, somewhere along the way, became your friends.

That's the whole film. The villain isn't a competitor — **we never name one.** The villain is the
*feeling* of building with AI today: the glow of an editor at 2 AM, red errors, the quiet despair of
"wait… wasn't this supposed to be simpler?" The hero is the most human thing imaginable — **a real
conversation with people you like.** That contrast is the sale.

## 🎵 SOUND DESIGN

- **0:00–0:24** — Sparse, melancholy solo piano, one repeating note. Diegetic: keyboard clacks
  (deliberately a little too loud, a little oppressive), a frustrated exhale, a clock ticking, distant city hum.
- **0:25–0:38** — Piano suspends on an unresolved chord. The clacking stops. A held silence on *"simpler?"* — then a single warm note blooms on *"what if."*
- **0:39–1:07** — Strings enter and build. The keyboard sounds are gone for good. In their place: the warm
  ambient murmur of a real meeting — light laughter, a few friendly voices.
- **1:08–1:33** — Full, elegant orchestral swell + subtle choir. Triumphant but never bombastic.
- **1:34–1:48** — Drops to one warm sustained chord under the logo. A single gold "shimmer" chime on the wordmark.
